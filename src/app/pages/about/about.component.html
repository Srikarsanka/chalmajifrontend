Late Sri Mattapalli Chalamayya

Founder & Chairman, Chalamaji Infra Projects Pvt. Ltd.

Late Sri Mattapalli Challamayya was a visionary industrialist, a pioneer in real estate, and a respected philanthropist in Visakhapatnam. As the Founder & Chairman of Chalamaji Infra Projects Pvt. Ltd., he played a pivotal role in shaping the group's evolution into a diversified enterprise.

Under his leadership, the organization expanded across multiple sectors, including trading, manufacturing, healthcare, construction, real estate, and irrigation, establishing a strong foundation built on quality, integrity, and long-term value creation.

He also served as Chairman of Jayalakshmi Agencies Pvt. Ltd. and held key leadership positions across several enterprises.

His enduring legacy continues to guide the group's commitment to excellence, ethical business practices, and meaningful contribution to society.
